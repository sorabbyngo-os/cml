# Alignment

Alignment ensures that intelligent behaviour remains consistent with the goals of Sorabbyngo OS.

Alignment differs from safety.

Safety answers:

"Can this action be executed safely?"

Alignment answers:

"Should this action be executed according to the user's intentions and system objectives?"

Alignment considers:

- User preferences
- Long-term objectives
- Organizational goals
- Ethical constraints
- System consistency

The Alignment Engine works closely with Chameha and Byngox during planning.
